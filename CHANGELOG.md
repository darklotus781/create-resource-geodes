# [0.2.0]
Updated for 1.21.1  

Introducing new items:  
- The Catalyst Agitator is a compound with mysterious properties, when deployed on a Catalyst, it causes new blocks to generate.  
- The Catalyst Core can be crafted once you've located a Catalyst Activator Wand, you can then use it to craft your own Catalysts.  

Added default recipes for Catalysts and Catalyst Activator Wand.  
The Catalysts can no longer be activated by redstone, instead you must craft Catalyst Agitator.  
The Catalyst Activator Wand may now be enchanted with Mending / Unbreaking to increase durability (base 65) so be careful with your crafting recipes!
